Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Holly Springs

Shut off the main valve

In most of Holly Springs' newer construction this is a lever valve near the water heater or where the line enters the garage. Turning it off stops the water damage from spreading while you wait.

Cut power to wet areas

A failing original water heater is the most common cause of a Holly Springs leak call. If the unit or nearby outlets are wet, shut off that circuit at the breaker panel if it is safe to do so.

Clear the immediate area

Move people, pets, and belongings away from an active leak or burst fitting until a technician arrives.
