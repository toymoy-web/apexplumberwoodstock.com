Slab Leak Detection & Repair in Woodstock, GA

This page focuses specifically on the slab leak scenario end to end: what causes it, how it's found, and what the repair actually involves. For a broader look at leak detection technology across every type of hidden leak, see our general leak detection page.

Warm Spot On The Floor Or A Meter That Won't Stop Moving?

Those two symptoms, an unexplained warm patch of flooring and a water meter that keeps spinning with everything in the house turned off, are the two most common ways a slab leak actually announces itself before anyone knows to call it that.

North Georgia's clay-heavy soil shifts more than sand or loam with seasonal moisture changes, and on slab-on-grade homes that shift is a real, recurring contributor to slab leaks, which is why this is consistently one of the highest-demand plumbing calls across our entire service area.

We use acoustic listening equipment and pressure testing to isolate a leak's location before any cutting happens. Acoustic detection listens for the specific sound of water escaping a pressurized line; pressure testing narrows down which section of the plumbing system is losing pressure. Together, they usually narrow a slab leak to a small, specific area.

Repair options depend on what detection finds. A single, isolated failure in an otherwise sound system is usually a straightforward spot repair, cutting into the slab at the specific point. A home with an aging galvanized system revealing one leak as an early sign of broader deterioration sometimes calls for rerouting a section of pipe above the slab instead of repeated concrete cutting.

Cost for the repair itself typically runs $900 to $2,800, on top of $300 to $900 for detection, with access difficulty (how deep, how central to the room) as the single biggest driver of where a specific job lands in that range.

Insurance coverage for a slab leak depends heavily on whether it's treated as sudden versus gradual damage, a real distinction that can determine whether a claim is approved. Documentation from the detection visit, how long the leak appears to have been active, matters for that conversation.

A repair performed under permit, required for most slab plumbing work in Cherokee County, is inspected against the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) adopted statewide in Georgia, part of why a proper repair costs more than an unlicensed, uninspected patch job.

A slab leak that goes undetected long enough can eventually affect the foundation itself, not just the plumbing, which is part of why addressing a suspected leak promptly matters beyond just the water bill and the immediate repair cost.

A second opinion is a reasonable step for a slab leak repair quote that seems unusually high, particularly for a proposed full reroute rather than a spot repair, since an independent technician reviewing the same detection findings can confirm or offer an alternative approach.

For leak types beyond slab leaks specifically, hidden wall leaks or underground lines between the meter and the house, see our broader leak detection page. If a slab leak turns out to originate at a water heater fitting rather than the slab itself, that changes the job into a simpler fix.

This is the single highest-demand call in Sixes and BridgeMill specifically, where the clay-soil risk combines with an established, 20-plus-year-old housing stock.

Sixes and BridgeMill see this call more than any other area in our service map, followed by the older, downtown-adjacent sections of Woodstock.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you find a slab leak without cutting into my floor?

Acoustic listening equipment picks up the sound of water escaping a pressurized line, and pressure testing narrows down which section is losing pressure. Together they isolate the leak's location before any cutting happens.

Is a slab leak repair or a full repipe the better choice?

It depends on whether detection reveals a single isolated failure or a sign of broader system deterioration. We walk through both options honestly based on what's actually found, not a default recommendation.

Is a slab leak covered by homeowners insurance?

It depends on whether it's treated as sudden or gradual damage, which varies by policy and carrier. Documentation of the leak's likely age from the detection visit matters for that determination.
