A Newer Community Just South Of Downtown

South on Main is a townhome and single-family community on Main Street, just south of Dupree Road. Public listing data shows homes built from 2015 to 2026. Its amenities include a pool, fitness center, clubhouse, tennis and pickleball courts, a dog park, and a playground.

That makes it very different from the 1990s neighborhoods on the west side of Woodstock. The plumbing here is young. Most of the problems we see in a young home are simple ones, and they are easy to fix when you call early.

Where Newer Homes Start To Need Attention

A tank water heater usually lasts 8 to 12 years. The earliest South on Main homes date to 2015. Their original tanks are now entering that window. If yours is making noise or running out of hot water sooner than it used to, call us before it fails. See water heater repair and water heater replacement.

If your home has a tankless unit, it still needs care. Tankless heaters collect mineral scale over time. Our tankless water heater service page explains what a service visit covers.

Drains in a new home still clog. Grease, wipes, and hair do not care how old the house is. A slow drain is worth a look early. See drain cleaning.

Builder Warranty, HOA, Or Plumber?

If your home is only a year or two old, check your builder warranty before you book a repair. Some defects may be covered. We are glad to tell you what we find, so you have it when you make a claim.

Community spaces are different. The pool, clubhouse, and shared grounds belong to the association. A leak there is a call for your HOA or property manager, not for us. Plumbing inside your own home is usually yours to handle, but your documents decide. Repairs follow the same statewide standard we work under in Woodstock, the 2018 International Plumbing Code with Georgia amendments. Read them if you are unsure.

Outdoor Lines In A Landscaped Community

Newer lots often have hose bibs and irrigation lines. On a hard freeze night, disconnect hoses and shut off the supply to outdoor spigots. A frozen outdoor line can split and leak inside the wall after it thaws. If you see a wet wall or a spike on your water bill, read about leak detection.

Emergency Calls In South on Main

Our dispatch line is open 24/7. For an active leak, see burst pipe repair. Call (770) 214-4545 and give us your street.

Nearby Areas

If You Have A Leak In Your South on Main Home

  • Close the main water valve for your home or unit.
  • Turn off the water heater’s power or gas if the leak is near it.
  • Move belongings away from the wet area and photograph the damage.
  • Check your builder warranty paperwork if the home is new.
  • Tell your HOA if water may be reaching a shared wall or common area.
  • Call (770) 214-4545 and we will send a plumber.

Frequently Asked Questions: South on Main

Do you serve the South on Main community?

Yes. We serve South on Main and the 30188 area around it. Call (770) 214-4545 and give us your street so we can route the nearest plumber.

My home is new. Should I call you or my builder?

Check your builder warranty first if the home is only a year or two old. If it is an emergency, shut off the water and call us. We can document what we find for your claim.

Does the HOA handle plumbing repairs?

Usually the association handles common areas such as the pool and clubhouse. Owners often handle the plumbing inside their own home. Your community documents decide, so check them.

Do you service tankless water heaters?

Yes. We service tankless units. Our tankless water heater service page explains what a visit covers.

Is emergency service available at night?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays.
