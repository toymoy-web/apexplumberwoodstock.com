All Plumbing Services in Woodstock, GA

Below is our full service list. If you don't see your specific issue listed, call directly; we handle the full range of residential and light commercial plumbing work across Woodstock and Cherokee County.

☎ Call (770) 214-4545

Water Heater Installation & Replacement

Full tank and tankless replacement, sized to your household's real hot water demand rather than a generic square-footage guess.

Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Cabling for straightforward clogs, hydro jetting for grease buildup and root intrusion, matched to what a technician actually finds in the line.

Leak Detection

Acoustic and pressure-based tracing to isolate a slab or hidden line leak before any cutting happens.

Water Heater Repair

Honest repair-versus-replace guidance based on the unit's age and the specific part that failed.

Tankless Water Heater Service

Descaling and flushing for on-demand units affected by this region's moderately hard water.

Sewer Line Repair

Camera-based diagnosis first, then a spot repair or trenchless fix scoped to what the camera actually shows.

Trenchless Pipe Lining

Fixing a damaged sewer line through a small access point instead of excavating your entire yard.

General Plumbing Repair

For anything that doesn't fit a single category: water hammer, valve repairs, and unexplained leaks or sounds.

Septic Tank Pumping

Real, regulated septic service for the well-and-septic properties in our more exurban coverage areas.

Commercial Plumbing

Grease trap service, multi-fixture restroom repair, and commercial water heater sizing for Woodstock-area businesses.

Repiping & Whole-Home Pipe Replacement

Whole-home or partial repiping with PEX or copper when an aging pipe system, not a single fixture, is the real problem.

Toilet Repair & Installation

Running, clogged, or leaking toilets repaired same-day; installation of customer-supplied units too.

Faucet & Sink Repair

Dripping faucets, low pressure at a single fixture, and slow sink drains, diagnosed and fixed without unnecessary replacement.

Slab Leak Detection & Repair

The full slab leak story: detection, repair options, and honest cost ranges for this region's highest-demand call.

Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation

Jam clearing, seal repair, and full replacement or first-time installation.

Hydro Jetting

Full-pipe cleaning for chronic clogs and root intrusion that cabling alone hasn't resolved.

Backflow Testing & Prevention

Certified annual testing for irrigation systems, pools, and commercial cross-connections.

Water Damage Restoration

24/7 extraction, drying, and structural restoration after a burst pipe or other water damage event.

Water Filtration Installation

Whole-home and point-of-use filtration and softening, tested and sized to your actual water, not a generic package.

Basement Waterproofing

Sump pumps, interior drainage, and exterior waterproofing for this region's clay soil and grading challenges.

Water Mitigation

The immediate first-response step that stops active water intrusion before it spreads further.
