A Lake City Across The County Line

Acworth sits in Cobb County on the shores of Lake Acworth and Lake Allatoona, about 10 miles from downtown Woodstock, and Bells Ferry Road ties the two cities together. It had 22,440 residents in 2020 and goes by the Lake City because Lake Acworth, a 362-acre lake, and the much larger Lake Allatoona border it.

Because Acworth is in Cobb County, utility accounts, permits, and inspections run through Cobb County and the City of Acworth rather than Cherokee County. The plumbing code is the same Georgia statewide standard, the 2018 International Plumbing Code with Georgia amendments. But the office you call for a permit is different.

One quirk is worth knowing. Addresses with an Acworth mailing address can sit in southwest Cherokee County, Bartow County, or Paulding County. We ask which county you are in before we dispatch.

Historic Downtown Homes And Lakeside Houses Need Different Calls

Acworth has more than 300 historic buildings, concentrated in the Historic Downtown District, the Mill District, and the Parkside District around Logan Farm Park. Older homes and buildings like these are where we find galvanized supply lines, cast iron drains. Original shutoff valves that no longer fully close.

A pressure drop or rusty water in one of them is often a pipe-material problem rather than a single bad fixture. A repiping conversation can be cheaper than a string of repairs.

Around Cauble Park and South Shore Park on Lake Acworth. The Dallas Landing and Proctor Landing shorelines on Lake Allatoona, lake proximity adds its own questions. Irrigation systems and outdoor spigots are common, and irrigation lines need annual backflow testing. Homes that sit low relative to the shoreline are the ones worth a sump pump and grading check.

Newer Neighborhoods, Aging Water Heaters

Acworth grew from about 5,800 residents in 1990 to 13,900 in 2000 and 22,400 by 2020. A large share of its homes were built in the 1990s and 2000s. Water heaters from that building wave are now 15 to 30 years old, which makes tank replacement one of the most common reasons Acworth homeowners call. We replace tanks and tankless units the same day when the unit is in stock.

Clay soil is the same on this side of Lake Allatoona as it is in Woodstock. A water bill that climbs with no visible cause is worth a leak detection visit before it turns into a slab or foundation problem.

Dispatch From Woodstock To Acworth, And The ZIPs We Cover

Acworth is usually a 20 to 30 minute drive from our Woodstock base depending on traffic on Highway 92 and Bells Ferry Road. The dispatch line is open around the clock. Same-day response is standard for an active leak, a sewage backup, or a failed water heater.

ZIP 30101 and ZIP 30102 together cover the city and the unincorporated areas that use an Acworth mailing address. If you are not sure which county you are in, give us the street address and we will confirm it. Neighbors in Towne Lake and Kennesaw are on the same dispatch loop.

Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Acworth

Know which valve controls the whole house

In older Acworth homes the main shutoff is often in a meter box near the street. In newer ones it is inside the garage or a utility closet. Close it fully before you call.

Protect what the water can reach

Lift furniture legs onto blocks, pull up rugs, and move electronics. In a basement or lower level, stay out of standing water until the power to that area is off.

Turn off the water heater if the leak is at the tank

Switch off its power or set the gas control to pilot, then close the cold-water inlet valve on top of the unit to stop the refill.
