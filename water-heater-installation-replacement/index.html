Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Woodstock, GA

Written by the Woodstock Apex Plumbing team · Updated October 11, 2026.

Most water heaters last 8 to 12 years, and Woodstock is now hitting the point where the units installed during the 2000s and early-2010s building boom are aging out at once. That is the real reason water heater replacement is the single highest-volume plumbing search in this city right now. We install and replace both tank and tankless units, same-day in most cases, with a written price before we start.

Emergency Water Heater Replacement When A Tank Fails

When a tank has rusted through or flooded a closet or garage, replacement cannot wait for next week. Shut off the cold water supply and the power or gas, and call our 24/7 emergency line so we can plan the swap.

Our emergency replacement service covers tank and tankless units. We confirm the size and fuel type, give you the price in writing, and install the new unit as quickly as the job allows.

A failing water heater rarely fails quietly. Rusty water, a tank that takes twice as long to heat, a puddle forming at the base, or a popping and rumbling sound from sediment buildup are the usual warning signs. Any one of them is worth a call before the unit fails completely and floods a garage or closet.

Georgia's moderately hard water accelerates sediment buildup inside a tank faster than it would in a soft-water region, which shortens a standard tank's working life if it has never been flushed. We check for that during every replacement estimate rather than assuming the tank simply reached a fixed age limit.

Tank versus tankless is a real decision, not just an upsell. A tankless unit costs more upfront but takes up a fraction of the space and does not run out of hot water during back-to-back showers. That matters more in the newer, larger homes going up around Towne Lake than it does in a smaller downtown bungalow with a single bathroom.

We install major manufacturers' tank and tankless units, including Rheem, A.O. Smith, and Rinnai, and haul away the old unit as part of the job. A Cherokee County permit is required for a water heater replacement. We pull it as part of the installation rather than leaving that step to the homeowner.

A gas tankless unit needs a dedicated vent sized for its output. In many cases a larger gas line than what fed a standard tank. It heats water on demand rather than storing a fixed amount. Converting from a tank to gas tankless in an older home sometimes means upgrading the gas line as part of the job, which we flag during the estimate rather than as a surprise mid-installation.

A tank water heater that is failing outright, rather than just aging normally, shows certain signs. These include water pooling at the base of the tank itself (not a nearby fitting), a persistent metallic or rotten-egg smell from the hot water, or water that looks rusty even after running the tap for a minute. Any of those is worth same-day attention rather than waiting for a full failure.

Expansion tank condition is worth checking during any water heater replacement in a home with a closed plumbing system (common where a check valve or pressure regulator is installed at the meter). A waterlogged expansion tank can cause pressure relief valve issues and premature tank wear that get misdiagnosed as an unrelated problem if nobody checks the small tank sitting next to the water heater.

A failing water heater and a slow drain often get blamed on each other when the real culprit is old galvanized supply line, which is why we check for that during a water heater repair visit even when the complaint sounds unrelated.

If the unit is leaking from a fitting rather than the tank itself, that is sometimes a leak detection call instead of a full replacement. We will tell you honestly which one it is before recommending the more expensive fix.

Homes in the newer Towne Lake subdivisions are the most common tankless-conversion candidates we see, simply because the larger floor plans put more demand on hot water at once.

The Eagle Watch, Towne Lake Hills, and Wyngate sections off Towne Lake Parkway, the Woodlands and Brookshire neighborhoods. Newer communities such as Bradshaw Farm all produce water heater calls for different reasons. Age drives the older sections, while demand drives the larger homes in the newer ones. Townhome communities near downtown Woodstock often mean a smaller closet install, so tell us the type of home when you call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a water heater replacement take in Woodstock?

A standard tank swap is usually finished same-day, often within two to three hours once the new unit is on the truck. A first-time tankless conversion can take longer if new gas or electrical lines are needed.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Cherokee County?

Yes. Cherokee County requires a permit for water heater replacement, and we handle pulling it as part of the installation.

Is it worth switching to tankless?

It depends on household size and hot water demand. Larger households that regularly run multiple showers or appliances at once tend to see the most benefit. A single-occupant downtown home may not recoup the higher upfront cost as quickly.
