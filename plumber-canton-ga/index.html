A County Seat With An Old Core And A New Growth Ring

Canton grew from 7,709 residents in 2000 to 22,958 in 2010 and 32,973 in 2020. The city is really two housing stocks sharing one river. Around the historic downtown, the 1929 white marble courthouse. The Etowah River Greenway at Heritage Park, you find older homes and commercial buildings whose supply lines and drain runs predate that growth.

Out along Riverstone Parkway and the I-575 interchanges, most of the newer subdivisions went up after 2000, which usually means PEX or copper supply lines rather than galvanized pipe.

That split decides what goes on the truck. A call near downtown Canton gets a technician ready for older pipe material and a camera for an aging sewer lateral. A call in a newer subdivision is more often a water heater in the 10 to 20 year range, a failed fill valve, or a clog that is really a fixture problem.

The Etowah River Runs Through The Middle, And It Matters Underground

The Etowah River flows east to west through the center of Canton. The creeks that feed it shape the grade of a lot of neighborhoods. Homes on low ground near the river corridor are where sump pumps, damp crawlspaces, and basement moisture come up most.

If a basement or crawlspace takes on water after a long rain, a quick sump pump check and a look at grading and downspouts usually comes before anything more invasive, and basement waterproofing is the next step if the pump alone is not the answer.

Georgia clay is part of the same story. Clay drains slowly, so water lingers against foundations and slabs long after a storm ends. On slab-on-grade homes the shifting clay is a recurring contributor to slab leaks. That is why leak detection is a common call in Canton too.

Water, Sewer, Or Septic: We Ask First

Inside city limits, Canton water and sewer typically come from the City of Canton. Many homes just outside the limits are served by the Cherokee County Water and Sewerage Authority. Some run on private wells and septic systems. Which one you have changes the repair, because a backup in a city-sewer home is a drain or lateral problem while a backup in a septic home may be a full tank or a failing drain field. When you call, tell us your address and who sends your water bill.

Before We Arrive: If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Canton

Find the shutoff before water spreads

Look for the main valve where the line enters the house. It is often in a garage or utility room, or at the meter box near the street for older homes close to downtown. Turn it clockwise to stop the flow.

Switch off power near standing water

If water is near outlets, a sump pump, or the water heater, turn off the breaker for that area before you step in. Never wade into water that could reach live wiring.

Move valuables off the floor

Lift rugs, boxes, and electronics out of the affected area and photograph the damage for your insurance claim before cleanup starts.
